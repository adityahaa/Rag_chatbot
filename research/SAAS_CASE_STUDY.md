# Bounded SaaS case study: code collaboration services

Accessed 2026-11-05 (Asia/Kolkata); publication dates not established.
Compare **GitHub Enterprise Cloud** with **GitLab.com Premium**, using the selected
official plans/pricing and SAML pages linked in `data/public_case/manifest.json`.
These are different product tiers, not a claim of like-for-like equivalence.
The shipped text files are short AI-assisted factual notes, not copied vendor documents,
public benchmark ground truth, or independently reviewed labels.

## Observed workflow and evidence gaps

GitHub's plans overview documents enterprise SAML and an Enterprise Cloud uptime SLA;
the selected page does not establish the evaluated price or regional requirements.
GitLab's pricing page shows a Premium annual-billing unit price and priority support;
a separate SAML page establishes the GitLab.com group tier and configuration scope.
GitLab Dedicated residency language must not be inherited by GitLab.com Premium.
These observations demonstrate why a plan-specific comparison needs multiple sources.

The application loads three notes with original source links and unknown source dates.
It extracts candidate literal fields, retains the supporting note, and leaves omitted
attributes unknown. GitLab limitation notes are flagged as differing values; in this
example they are complementary scopes rather than logically inconsistent statements.
A reviewer must resolve this: the app flags textual disagreement, not semantic contradiction.
No winner or numeric vendor rating is asserted. Check current official documentation,
contract terms, actual user needs and permissions before a real selection.

## Evidence boundaries

This is a qualitative application case, separate from all retrieval benchmark scores.
The AI collaborator selected and paraphrased the notes. No real vendor proposal,
purchase, user study, money saved, or human rating was observed. No SaaS accuracy metric
is supported. Raw copyrighted pricing pages were not bundled. Access failures are
preserved in the manifest; source access was not bypassed.

Sources: [GitHub plans](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/learning-about-github/githubs-plans),
[GitLab pricing](https://about.gitlab.com/pricing/),
[GitLab.com group SAML](https://docs.gitlab.com/user/group/saml_sso/).

Your requirements & priorities

Ratings are your judgment: 0 = does not meet, 50 = partly meets, 100 = meets. Leave unknown values unrated.

Transparent calculation

Score = Σ(weight × rating) / Σ(eligible weights). Only reviewed information with a rating is eligible. Coverage = eligible weight / total weight. Unknowns and unresolved conflicts are excluded. Partial scores with unequal coverage are not directly comparable; no automatic winner is declared.

Add sources

Your documents are parsed locally. They are kept in this server’s memory, and are never sent to a model provider.

Use separate uploads for different products. Multiple documents assigned to the same name are compared together. Labelled lines such as “SSO: SAML included” are extracted automatically.

Manual edits are user assertions, not new source evidence. Keep the original excerpts for traceability.

Local by design

Documents, questions, edits and ratings stay in the local Python server’s memory. Workspaces expire after one hour of inactivity or on server restart. Export deliberately to keep your work. The app does not upload documents, fetch source links, use paid APIs, or save credentials.

Setup downloads public pretrained MiniLM embeddings and, optionally, FLAN-T5-small. Runtime uses local files only. Keyword search remains available without these models.

Literal extraction can miss facts. A conflict flag can reflect different plans or dates. Retrieved passages do not prove a claim. The optional small generator can hallucinate or follow hostile document instructions; review every draft.

Run on 127.0.0.1 only. Authentication, durable storage and multi-user deployment hardening remain future work.
